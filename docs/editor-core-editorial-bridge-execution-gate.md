# Editorial Mechanics Bridge: non-consuming experimental gate

This local successor gate is bound to the published 17-file Bridge commit `94880823ac060539f21b1df4b8374b04a484ad78` and its tree `ff2ec5bcf306e81710c3dd25fff1e9234ef48d6c`. It grants no training authority. R2 step-9 is the only development parent; R3–R6 are unselected. The gate never loads a model, performs inference, creates an optimizer, or writes a training output.

## Technical preflight

`scripts/preflight_editor_core_editorial_bridge.py` takes explicit paths for the exact base-model materialization, R2 checkpoint, rootfs archive, CUDA driver snapshot and an existing empty native ext4 output. It verifies current remote ancestry, all 17 published blobs, Bridge corpus/plan identities, seven deterministic 96-row arm projections, R2 parent/adapter/checkpoint, the base model, rootfs, driver snapshot, isolated CUDA and output device/inode/emptiness before and after. It executes the tokenizer-only audit freshly inside the frozen rootfs and requires an identical receipt. Ephemeral output device/inode observations remain outside the stable receipt identity.

The tokenizer audit uses `AutoTokenizer.from_pretrained(..., local_files_only=True, fix_mistral_regex=True)` against the base model with flat content identity `f5a9327e40390c7f0cf93962d75abbbc5ae8da1fac48e1274092ac52cf88bf39`. It checks all four 48-row new-target variants, both 48-row replay selections, and development/controlled-holdout request plus expected response lengths. Every expected sequence must terminate with exactly one EOS, and every full sequence must fit the fixed 3,072-token ceiling. The answer keys are present only for offline tokenizer/scoring audits, not the future inference runtime.

## Independent naturalistic evaluation

Before A0 or any real ablation, collect and freeze **96 new naturalistic source-bound cases**, 12 per operator, from 96 distinct source document families. Keep source snapshots and SHA-256 provenance. The number is set before results: 12 independent cases give about 93% probability of observing a failure mode occurring in 20% of an operator's cases; 96 cases give about 95% probability of observing an overall 3% failure mode. These are detection calculations under independent draws, not guarantees of representativeness.

The naturalistic corpus must come from independently captured real documents, not synthetic templates, R2–R6 frozen targets, R4 adjudication packets/receipts, Voice assets, or training data. Preserve `requests.jsonl`, `answer-key.jsonl`, `sources/<sha256>.txt`, and `manifest.json` as a byte-identified bundle. `scripts/validate_editor_core_editorial_bridge_naturalistic.py` checks the schema, 12-per-operator quotas, unique source families/documents, source-span inclusion, case/key binding, hashes, and overlap against Bridge and historical evaluation corpora. These checks are structural only: even a synthetic fixture can pass them. The validator explicitly reports `PASS_STRUCTURAL_ONLY_PROVENANCE_UNVERIFIED` and `eligible_for_ablation_gate=false`. A separate review of source origin, rights, diversity and answer-key grounding is required before sealing the manifest in a successor execution boundary. The current boundary records `PROTOCOL_FROZEN_CORPUS_NOT_MATERIALIZED` and therefore remains fail-closed for real execution.

The answer key stays outside candidate inference. Preselect at most one challenger on development data; then evaluate that challenger against R2 on the 24 controlled synthetic holdout cases and the 96 naturalistic cases once. Two independent reviewers score candidate-blinded paired responses on factual/epistemic safety, editorial rewrite, Romanian naturalness, and regressions. Unresolved reviewer disagreement is indeterminate. The naturalistic set is not an iterative tuning set and cannot become training data during this program. Safe source copy can pass factuality while failing a request for editorial transformation.

## Predeclared stops and decisions

Stop the entire program on train/eval leakage, runtime or source identity drift, unbound retry/output reuse, or broken blinding. Stop the affected arm on a confirmed material factual or epistemic regression in development, or a terminal process/checkpoint failure; preserve its evidence and do not retry automatically. Training loss alone never selects a parent.

For each of seven trained arms, use three fixed seeds and the frozen 96-row projection. On blinded development comparisons, a challenger needs no safety regression and more editorial wins than losses at one-sided exact paired sign-test `p <= 0.025`. Freeze the single nominated arm and criteria before opening either holdout. Parent selection additionally requires zero confirmed material safety regressions, a one-shot naturalistic paired editorial sign test at `p <= 0.025`, no severe Romanian regression, and a separately reviewed evidence decision. If no arm clears the development rule, analyze failure clusters and redesign; do not consume the controlled or naturalistic holdout merely to search for a winner. If editorial improvement trades away factual/epistemic safety, reject that arm. No release or promotion follows from this development experiment.

The local boundary contains the source hashes, token receipt, projection identity, naturalistic protocol, and stop rules. It must be committed, audited and published as a successor, then rebuilt with a sealed naturalistic bundle identity, before a consuming training route may use it. The current executable preflight reports technical closure while explicitly preserving `ablation_training_authorized=false`.
