# VNext VOICE Mechanism Selection Diagnostic v1

This design-only diagnostic compares the three authorized choices without materializing a VOICE runtime or changing the VNext product lock.

The legacy deterministic executor proves strong integrity, abstention and repetition-control principles. Its useful realization path is not a clean `EditorSetupPacket` consumer: it is bound to fact atoms, semantic draft and event authority identities, eligibility, owner selection, repetition snapshots, activation allowlists and governed persisted context. Removing those dependencies leaves fixed generic surfaces or frozen-fixture reproduction, neither of which demonstrates useful open-domain Pastila Acidă commentary. Option A is therefore stopped as the general VNext mechanism.

A model-based commentary-only realizer is the best target architecture. It must receive immutable setup bytes plus bounded style instructions, emit commentary in a separate field, and preserve abstention. No candidate, acquisition, runtime or quality evidence is authorized or claimed here. Model selection and dependency acquisition require a separate owner gate.

Until that gate closes, VOICE remains outside VNext. This permits continued work on the factual development closure but blocks integrated acceptance and `GLOBAL VNEXT MIGRATION PASS`.
