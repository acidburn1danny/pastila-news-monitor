# VNext VOICE factual-safety taxonomy and constrained projection design

The boundary separates exact factual copies, entailed derivations, semantic equivalents, and unsupported facts. Numeric surface inequality alone is not factual drift. The classifier is deliberately bounded and cannot replace general claim extraction.

The future runtime projection must operate after candidate generation and before `VOICE_DRAFT` persistence. It binds every extracted claim to the immutable factual artifact, allows supported copies, derivations with explicit proof, and normalized semantic equivalents, and rejects unsupported claims. A rejected response receives at most one regeneration under the same authority; a second rejection becomes `ABSTAIN`. The orchestrator remains the only workflow writer.

This design reduces operational risk but does not alter model weights. General entity, event, status, attribution, and temporal claims require typed extractors in the implementation boundary. VOICE remains `DISABLED_UNTIL_PROMOTION`.
