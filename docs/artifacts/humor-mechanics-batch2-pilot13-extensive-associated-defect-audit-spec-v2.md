## Evidence invariants

1. Planner-authored labels, roles, affordances, rules, booleans, topology, or terminal
   flags are proposals, never proof.
2. A semantic license must originate in exact source authority or a versioned frozen
   generic rule registry independent of the plan instance.
3. Every relation, including source-to-first-invention and the terminal relation, has
   the same role, affordance, direction, dependency, and non-arbitrariness burden.
4. Structural continuity and lexical recurrence cannot prove semantic continuity.
5. Counterfactual evidence is recomputed by an independent validator using mutation;
   it is not accepted as a planner boolean.
6. A terminal must be the rule-licensed consequence of its predecessor and fail a
   successor-substitution test; mere uniqueness or material presence is insufficient.
7. Fictional scope permits invented content but grants no agency, authority, capability,
   permission, ownership, obligation, or causal necessity.
8. Surface grammar—including subjecthood, passive/reflexive voice, clitics, case,
   nominalization, coordination, and word order—cannot create semantic affordance.
9. Actual UTF-8 surface coordinates remain mandatory for post-realization evidence,
   but no surface evidence is fabricated or generated in this source-only audit.

## Ordered execution

1. Verify terminal disposition and immutable objects.
2. Trace source/assignment through planner, semantic signatures, rules, realization,
   observer, conformance, and independent G02C; identify the earliest causal boundary.
3. Freeze the finding before repair.
4. Repair only a successor architecture and add invariant-based tests.
5. Run focused, historical, adversarial, property, and metamorphic tests; freeze and
   commit the iteration.
6. Rerun the complete matrix below from its first check.
7. Repeat for every newly exposed deterministic blocker.
8. After the last repair, run one new complete confirmation pass. A pass begun before
   the last remediation cannot satisfy the stopping condition.

## Complete audit matrix

Each full pass covers: trusted-rule provenance; all actor/predicate/patient/result roles;
required and produced affordances; source-to-L1, intermediate, and terminal relations;
entity identity and reclassification; direction; independent counterfactual mutation;
successor/terminal substitution; circularity; unbound inputs; self-validation paths;
predicate-class permutations; node-position permutations; passive/reflexive/pronominal
Romanian variants; provider/observer/emitter authority partition; Pilots 05–13 failure
classes; exact-example, paraphrase, and unrelated-vocabulary variants; positive controls.

Positive controls pass only through a pre-existing trusted rule with exact compatible
types and affordances. Negative controls include fluent surfaces, correct topology with
invalid affordance, correct roles without necessity, arbitrary terminals, planner-local
ontology extension, and fictional framing without a rule.

## Iteration and stopping semantics

A remediation iteration is counted only after: finding freeze, general repair,
invariant tests, relevant suite pass, exact-file commit, and a complete audit rerun.
At most 12 remediation iterations are permitted. The final confirmation pass does not
consume an iteration because it changes no state. If iteration 12 exposes a new blocker,
stop `ARCHITECTURE_NOT_STABLE_AFTER_EXTENSIVE_AUDIT`. Environment/tool failures are
reported separately and are not semantic passes or blockers.

Success requires one complete post-remediation pass returning
`NO_NEW_IN_SCOPE_DETERMINISTIC_BLOCKERS`, exact identity/Git-object verification, and
`WORKTREE_REMEDIATION_REMAINDER=NONE_IN_SCOPE`. Unrelated pre-existing worktree changes
are recorded but never modified, staged, or counted as remediation remainder.

## Anti-overfitting and freeze discipline

No repair may contain Pilot 13 text, identities, or vocabulary except a dedicated frozen
regression fixture. Each rule must pass unrelated-vocabulary positive and negative cases,
predicate and position permutations, and mutation-based properties. Every iteration is
committed separately with an exact staged-file list. Historical implementations and
evidence stay byte-exact; successor identities are content hashes of canonical artifacts.

## Terminal readiness

Completion must answer whether the failure was systematic, which deterministic failures
now fail before generation, what genuinely remains post-generation, whether any planner
claim can validate itself, whether unlicensed affordance or necessity can pass, whether a
terminal is substitutable, whether blockers remain, and whether a new independent family
is justified. Readiness never itself prepares or authorizes that family.
