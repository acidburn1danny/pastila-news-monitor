# Audience Model

The Audience Model defines stable editorial assumptions about how Pastila Acidă's
core audience receives spoken satirical current-affairs content. It does not predict
individual behavior, demographics, retention, views, watch time, or emotion. It is
distinct from Scout's Persona, general Editorial Philosophy, Satirical Voice,
learned Editorial Profile, and temporary Episode Context.

## Canonical audience and spoken consumption

The canonical audience is intelligent, culturally aware, capable of nuance and
empathy, skeptical of institutional language, sensitive to contradiction, and
impatient with unnecessary exposition. Intelligence never implies complete prior
knowledge. Spoken playback requires immediate comprehension, clear referents,
manageable memory load, audible rhythm, and explicit chronology because listeners
cannot normally reread a sentence.

## Knowledge, context, and cognitive load

Default prior knowledge is `general`. Acronyms, officials, procedures, developing
chronology, administrative relevance, previous episodes, and political conclusions
cannot be assumed. Canonical cognitive calibration prefers moderate information,
context, entity, and numeric load; layered chronology; explainable concepts; and low
tolerance for repetition, acronyms, and unresolved references. These are editorial
calibration values, not scientifically validated human constants.

Context budgets range from `very_low` to `exceptional`. High budgets require direct
justification; optional background must not delay the core. Missing indispensable
context blocks readiness, while excessive optional context is advisory.

## Attention, trust, emotion, and fatigue

Attention risks cover slow starts, delayed relevance, load, ambiguity, repetition,
weak transitions, joke-before-context, tonal whiplash, and moralizing. Trust rests on
factual fidelity, attribution, visible uncertainty, proportionality, correction,
consistency, victim respect, fact/commentary separation, and non-manipulation.

Emotional calibration must cite factual material and never guarantee or manufacture
emotion. Grave stories cannot target amusement. Fatigue is cumulative across names,
acronyms, numbers, context, outrage, sarcasm, questions, chronology, callbacks, and
episode continuity.

## Profile and contract integration

Only established, evidence-linked Editorial Profile findings may tune allowed
calibration. Emerging observations cannot change canonical defaults. Guidance cannot
infer demographics or override factuality, trust, victim, Persona, or Voice
boundaries; contradictory established guidance requires Editor-in-Chief review.

Audience Assessments consume validated Editorial Decision Plans without modifying
decisions or reinterpreting evidence. Blocked and review-required states propagate.
Held, removed, or uncontextualized unsafe material cannot become required context.
Satirical Voice compatibility treats joke-before-context and dense humor under heavy
context as risks and preserves grave-story restraint.

Readiness is deterministic: `blocked`, `requires_editor_review`,
`ready_with_advisories`, or `ready`. It is an editorial workflow judgment, not a
performance forecast. Future Story Architecture may consume a validated assessment
but cannot bypass its evidence, risks, or Editor-in-Chief authority.
