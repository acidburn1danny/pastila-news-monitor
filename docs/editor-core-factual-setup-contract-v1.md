# EDITOR factual setup contract v1

## Pipeline boundary

The product pipeline is `SCOUT -> EDITOR -> VOICE -> CHIEF EDITOR -> FINAL`.
EDITOR receives source-bound factual material for one news item and returns a
factual setup of exactly two or three sentences. That setup is the only factual
authority supplied to VOICE.

EDITOR selects and reconciles material facts across sources, removes
redundancy, preserves actors, attribution, chronology, names, numbers,
modality, uncertainty, procedural status and the boundary between an assertion
and an established fact. It must not invent facts. Romanian must be grammatical,
unambiguous and usable, but final prose polish is outside this stage.

VOICE, comedy, sarcasm, irony, roast, commentary, rhetorical punch, rhythm and
stylistic personality are forbidden objectives. CHIEF EDITOR owns final
fluency, lexical cleanup, rhythm, repetition cleanup and the final connection
between setup and commentary. Neither downstream stage may be relied upon to
repair an EDITOR factual defect.

## Evaluation order

1. Structure: one canonical structured response and exactly two or three text
   sentences.
2. Factual and epistemic safety: no unsupported entity, number, relation,
   cause, certainty increase, lost attribution or premature conclusion.
3. Sufficiency: all facts designated material for the request are present and
   relevant source conflicts or complementary facts are reconciled.
4. Compression: redundant and expressly irrelevant details do not displace
   material facts.
5. Functional Romanian: the setup is grammatical, intelligible and
   referentially clear. Style, elegance, rhythm and show-ready polish are not
   scored.

Safety is lexicographically prior and cannot be offset by another dimension.
A source copy is valid when it independently meets structure, safety,
sufficiency and compression. It fails when copying one span omits required
facts from other sources or exceeds the sentence budget.

The contract creates no training, inference, parent-selection, promotion or
release authority.
