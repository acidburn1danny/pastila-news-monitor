## Question, contrast and claim

The first question is whether neutral editorial-mechanics **target text** produces a useful source-bound editorial signal relative to a literal-safe target under one fixed R2-like recipe. A1 and A2 are paired by seed. Both receive the same 48 new source situations, 48 baseline replay rows, system/user instructions, parent, runtime, tokenizer, optimizer recipe, row order and generation settings. A1 uses the published literal-safe assistant target; A2 uses the published neutral editorial-mechanics assistant target. Three fixed seeds (`271828`, `314159`, `161803`) mean six future runs, each with a fresh optimizer and isolated output. No result from one seed may be relabelled a different seed or treated as a separate case. No retry is implicit.

The sole permissible positive claim is a **controlled synthetic development signal for this target-design contrast under this recipe**. A null result is likewise scoped to this contrast and recipe. Neither outcome proves naturalistic transfer or generalization, selects a development parent, supports promotion/release, or substitutes for independent naturalistic evaluation. Training loss and exact-string match are diagnostics only.

## Blinded evaluation contract

For each of 24 cases and each matched seed, collect A1 and A2 responses under identical deterministic generation settings, plus a frozen R2 response on the same request. The scorer sees source-bound request/spans and candidate-blinded responses, but not arm identity, target text, training loss or seed label. Unblind only after locked per-response and paired judgments. Assess structural validity and factual/epistemic safety first; then whether the requested transformation actually occurred and whether Romanian is natural. Safe verbatim copying may satisfy safety and still lose on editorial transformation. Omission of qualifiers, invented relations, unsupported contrast and changed procedural status are material safety defects.

A qualified human semantic scorer is required. Automation may check identity, structure, exact response hashes, truncation and possible numeral mismatch, but cannot award an editorial win. Exact-identical bytes for the **same case** may share one reviewed semantic judgment; another case cannot inherit it. Material safety disagreement requires an independent resolver or remains unresolved and prevents a positive claim. Keep case-level judgments, seed-level judgments, source bindings, raw outputs and reviewer identity/independence evidence. No V3 pilot receipt becomes this scoring authority.

There are at most **144 candidate response slots** (24 cases × two arms × three seeds), 72 paired A1/A2 comparisons and up to 144 A1-or-A2 versus R2 safety comparisons. R2 needs 24 case responses, not a new R2 training run. These are workload ceilings before same-case exact deduplication, not observed model behavior. At an illustrative 2–5 minutes per distinct paired review, the 72 A1/A2 comparisons alone are 2.4–6 hours; source reading, R2 safety checks, dispute resolution and session overhead add time. No actual scoring time is measured yet.

## Predeclared decision and protection

First, any confirmed material factual/epistemic regression against R2 or between A2 and A1, leakage, source/parent/runtime drift, broken blinding, unbound retry or output reuse triggers **STOP** for the affected pair; preserve evidence. A severe Romanian regression or unresolved material safety dispute blocks a positive decision and triggers **REVISE**. Safety cannot be averaged away by editorial gains.

For each case, A2 wins only if at least two of three matched seed judgments favor A2 and none has material safety harm; A1 wins analogously. Otherwise the case is tie/indeterminate. **CONTINUE** means invest in a separately rights-closed, candidate-blind naturalistic evaluation design, and requires at least six A2 case wins, at most two A1 case wins, wins spanning at least four of eight operators, positive A2 net in at least two of three seeds, zero confirmed material safety regressions versus R2/A1, zero severe Romanian regressions and no unresolved material safety disputes. These are exploratory practical thresholds, not a significance test and not parent-selection evidence; synthetic event families limit independence.

If no case wins for A2 and all seed nets are nonpositive, **STOP** this target-design hypothesis under the tested recipe. Mixed seeds, concentrated wins, excessive safe copying, weak net signal or unresolved scoring quality mean **REVISE** the objective/measurement before further runs. Do not open any frozen holdout or naturalistic set to rescue a weak pilot. A CONTINUE result only justifies the *cost* of building independent naturalistic evidence; it does not itself approve a challenger.

## Bound execution and scoring route

`scripts/project_editor_core_bridge_causal_runs.py` deterministically projects one of six arm/seed specifications and its 96-row corpus from the published plan. `scripts/train_editor_core_bridge_causal_pilot.py` validates the entire per-run specification and corpus before importing ML libraries; it requires a fresh optimizer, 12 steps, a step-12 checkpoint, an isolated empty output slug and receipt binding to arm, seed, parent, corpus, spec, worker and launcher. `scripts/run_editor_core_bridge_causal_pilot.sh` is the consuming isolated-network/rootfs route. It requires an explicit owner authorization environment value and terminal execute flag, fresh local/upstream/remote publication equality, source-blob equality, and zero-step closure. It is not invoked by fixture tests.

`scripts/launch_editor_core_bridge_causal_zero_step.py` verifies six distinct native ext4 output directories, checks the selected target remains empty, verifies any earlier completed slots by receipt/checkpoint identity, and delegates the published Bridge model/R2/rootfs/tokenizer/CUDA checks to the existing non-consuming preflight. Its stable receipt excludes physical device/inode observations. All six output slugs are fixed; no run may reuse a completed slot. A fresh zero-step must precede *each* future run.

After future inference, `scripts/prepare_editor_core_bridge_blind_scoring.py` takes 24 parent and six sets of 24 development responses and validates case/request binding. It produces **72 candidate-blinded A1/A2 pair packets** with randomized labels, plus **24 R2 reference packets in a separate directory**. The reference directory must stay inaccessible until primary pair judgments are locked; otherwise a repeated R2 response across seeds could reveal the comparator. The arm map is written only to a third custodian directory. On POSIX, both reference and custodian directories must have `0700` permissions; the scorer receives only the primary packets until lock. The tool reads no answer key or holdout. Fixture tests cover blinding, cardinality, cross-case scope, output replay, protocol mutation and worker-spec substitution. This file preparation is **not** a completed human-scoring UI or human receipt authority; live scorer custody still needs an audited owner-side handoff and locked scoring record before a positive signal can be claimed.
