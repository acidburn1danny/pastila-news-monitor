# Editorial Mechanics Bridge: staged A1 versus A2 design

**Status: DESIGN ONLY.** This design does not authorize training, optimizer steps, naturalistic panel production, reviewer handoff, parent change, adjudication, promotion or release. It leaves the historical 96-case protocol and the separate proposed 16+48 protocol intact. A new reviewed/published execution boundary would be necessary before any real A1/A2 run. R2 step-9 (`c680d686f0b139e618d99fab235c62267caa9482df0efbccb44cb2c58c124f02`) remains the development parent; R3–R6 are unselected.

## Causal question and intervention

Stage one asks whether **editorial-mechanics target design**, holding every other training factor fixed, changes source-bound editorial behavior more usefully than a literal-safe target. A1 receives the literal-safe control target; A2 receives the neutral editorial-mechanics target. Each matched A1/A2 pair uses the same 48 source situations, 48 baseline replay slots, operator-specific request, R2 parent, base model, runtime, row order per seed, one-epoch R2-like recipe, optimizer configuration, tokenizer ceiling and deterministic inference settings. The three published seeds are `271828`, `314159`, `161803`. Fresh optimizer state is required for every future run; no arm inherits another arm. The *only assigned factor* is the target text. The comparison is A2 versus A1 within each seed and case, with R2 as an additional safety reference. It estimates the effect of these target choices under this one recipe, not the general effect of all editorial mechanics, a stronger recipe, or future production behavior.

Before execution, audit that generated arm projections differ only in the intended target bytes and bound identities. Any source, replay, instruction, token budget, runtime, parent, seed or generation mismatch destroys the causal contrast and stops the arm pair. Training loss, exact target match and synthetic-only wins are diagnostics, not a positive signal.

## Diagnostic set and independent review

Use **16 new real-document cases**, two per Bridge operation, from 16 distinct document/event families. Require at least four independent publishers, four genres, 12 demonstrably original-Romanian documents, two speeches/interviews and two genuine multi-position records; no publisher contributes more than six. These are minimum diversity checks for diagnosing transfer, not claims of prevalence. Pair them with the existing 24 synthetic *development* cases. Frozen synthetic holdout, historical R2–R6 holdouts, V1–V3 pilots, R4 adjudication and Voice/comedy remain outside training and this diagnostic set.

Each real case needs document-scope lawful local use, source identity, exact raw capture and deterministic extraction, PDF page/text fidelity if applicable, original-language evidence, source-family separation, exact UTF-8 byte-offset spans, a bounded request, author-sealed provisional key and anti-contamination closure. An independent Romanian key reviewer gives a locked blind first pass before seeing the author key; material disagreement goes to another independent resolver or case exclusion. A local hash/identity receipt route is possible for development research but must first be explicitly bound and audited; V3 pilot receipts cannot be reused as case authority. If the 16 cannot be admitted, **no A1/A2 run begins**. Source copies or public URLs do not imply reuse rights. No frozen answer key enters inference.

A qualified independent scorer, blind to arm identity and target text, scores every *distinct, same-case* response pair needed for nomination on factual/epistemic safety, requested editorial transformation and Romanian naturalness. R2 is shown under a neutral label as a safety comparator. A second independent reviewer resolves any material safety dispute; if unresolved, the case contributes no editorial win and blocks a positive safety conclusion. Exact byte-identical outputs for the same case can reuse an already audited verdict; similarities or identical text on different cases cannot. Automation may detect truncation, identical bytes and possible missing numbers for human attention, but cannot award a semantic win.

## Predeclared stage-one decision

For each of the 16 naturalistic cases, combine the three seed-level A2-versus-A1 decisions into a **single case-level sign**: win if at least two seeds favor A2 and no material safety harm exists; loss if at least two favor A1; otherwise tie/indeterminate. This avoids treating three outputs for one source as three independent documents. Report every seed and case, including unchanged outputs and source copies. On the 24 synthetic development cases report the same inventory separately; no pooling with naturalistic cases for the exploratory sign test.

**EXPAND** to a later causal contrast only if all identity, rights, review and contamination gates pass; A2 has positive editorial net wins on the naturalistic cases in at least two of three seeds; at least four naturalistic cases are case-level A2 wins; the one-sided exact paired sign test on resolved discordant *case-level* signs has `p <= 0.10`; **neither A1 nor A2** has a confirmed material factual/epistemic regression versus R2, A2 has none versus A1, neither has a severe Romanian regression, and no material review dispute remains unresolved. This prevents an unsafe A1 from creating an artificial relative gain for A2. Four wins and zero losses give `p=0.0625`; three wins and zero losses give `p=0.125` and do not expand. This is a deliberately exploratory expansion rule, **not** a parent-selection test or a generalization claim. Synthetic development must show no contradictory material safety result; a synthetic-only editorial gain cannot trigger expansion.

**STOP** the affected run/pair and preserve evidence on a confirmed material safety regression, leakage, altered source/model/runtime identity, broken blinding, unbound retry or invalid receipt. Stop the target-design hypothesis under this recipe if at least two seeds show no naturalistic editorial wins and no source-bound case-level positive signal, with the diagnostic set otherwise valid. Do not open the confirmatory panel to rescue a weak result.

**REVISE before any further runs** if the signal is mixed across seeds, confined to synthetic development, dependent on disputed keys, or too small for the exploratory threshold; if the 16-case set cannot meet the admission/diversity gates; or if review cost is disproportionate. Revision may change the objective or supply strategy, but requires a newly predeclared design and an untouched confirmation set. It is not permission for retrying A1/A2 on the same evaluation cases until a win appears.

If EXPAND passes, the next informative branch is a *matched* stronger-recipe A3-versus-A4 contrast; compare A1/A3 and A2/A4 to distinguish recipe strength and interaction. A5 protective replay or A6/A7 generic-instruction contrasts require separate branch evidence and predeclared tests; they do not start automatically. Expansion means more research, not selection of A2 or any other Core parent.

## Confirmatory custody and later parent selection

Before stage-one outputs exist, freeze the **selection procedure and strata** for a separate 48-case naturalistic confirmatory panel, six per operation. Its curator must not see A1/A2 or later candidate outputs. Actual source capture and key review may occur later, but the 48 source/key identities must be closed before first confirmatory inference. The panel must remain disjoint by source/event family from development, training, synthetic holdout, pilot and historical evidence. It cannot tune targets, recipe, replay, arm choice or stop rules. If its curator learns candidate results or the selection procedure changes after results, the panel is invalid and needs a genuinely new independently selected set.

The later development-parent decision requires a single challenger nominated from development under a separately frozen rule, the untouched 24-case synthetic controlled holdout, the one-shot 48-case naturalistic comparison, two independent candidate-blinded response reviewers, zero confirmed material factual/epistemic regressions, no severe Romanian regression, an exact one-sided paired editorial sign test at `p <= 0.025`, and a separate evidence review. Stage-one `p <= 0.10` cannot replace that gate. Even a later PASS permits only development-parent consideration, never release or promotion. R2 remains parent unless that stronger evidence closes.

## Workload and feasibility

Six possible stage-one runs × 40 development cases give **240 raw response-case pairs**. Same-case exact-hash equivalence can reduce the human semantic workload, but no real outputs exist to measure how much. At an illustrative 2–5 minutes per distinct changed pair, the upper bound is 8–20 scorer-hours; key review for 16 real cases, dispute resolution and source reading add time. Rights/provenance/curation can dominate calendar effort, and the existing pool has not demonstrated two rights-closed speeches and two multi-position cases. These are planning ranges, not observed times. A fixture-only projection should test the deduplication and reviewer UI before live work; a rights-cleared conversion sample must demonstrate that the 16-case diagnostic set is feasible before training authorization.

## Claims and adversarial checks

An EXPAND result supports only: "under the matched R2-like recipe, A2 shows an exploratory editorial signal over A1 on this 16-case diagnostic panel without a material regression detected here." It cannot establish population prevalence, rare-error absence, superiority to R2 as a development parent, or release safety. A STOP result falsifies this *specific* target-design/recipe combination at the detectable effect size; it does not prove editorial mechanics cannot ever work.

The design must reject: source/replay/recipe drift between arms; seed pooling as independent documents; a safe copy scored as an editorial rewrite; synthetic wins used to override naturalistic losses; a material safety regression averaged away; unlicensed third-party source text; an author-key leak into blind review; reuse of the 48 for design; multiple challenger openings; and pilot receipts promoted to live authority. These are hard gates in a future executable boundary, not tests claimed to have run against models now.

**Current closure:** protocol design only; 16-case admission, reviewer route, confirmatory selection and training authority remain blocked. No A1/A2, optimizer step, human review, adjudication, promotion or release occurred.
