# Romanian Conversational Engine

The Romanian Conversational Engine is the deterministic `ro-RO` realization-policy layer downstream of the language-neutral Spoken Communication Engine. Its canonical identity is `pastila-acida-romanian-conversational-engine` version `1.0.0`.

It classifies whether bounded constructions are plausible in contemporary spoken Romanian while preserving first-hearing clarity, evidence, attribution, legal status, sensitivity, Persona, Voice, Audience, Story Architecture, and communication constraints. It is not a complete grammar, lexicon, sociolinguistic prediction, language generator, or correction-learning system.

## Naturalness and register

Conversational authenticity is independent from grammatical correctness. A grammatically possible construction may be literary, bureaucratic, translated, theatrical, or socially implausible. Colloquial form does not automatically establish authenticity.

The preferred register precedence is neutral conversational, polished conversational, informal conversational, then restrained colloquial. Familiar, street-influenced, formal, legal, and internet-native registers are context-dependent. Ceremonial, journalistic, bureaucratic, academic, and performative-slang registers are discouraged as defaults. Register acceptability remains story-, severity-, Persona-, Voice-, and Audience-specific.

## Romanian policy surface

The engine supplies bounded policies for spoken syntax, natural and marked word order, inversion, recoverable ellipsis, functional fragments, purposeful repetition, connector families, lexical naturalness, colloquial language, slang, jargon, translated constructions, press language, bureaucratic nominalization and hidden agency, academic framing, and legal precision.

Entity shortening must preserve identity. Demonstratives must resolve unambiguously and dismissive forms require Voice and sensitivity compatibility. Emphasis and Romanian rhythm realize upstream communication timing without punctuation or performance notation. Controlled repair may improve authenticity but cannot manufacture mistakes, change facts, or add information.

Satire remains attached to upstream Satirical Opportunities and Story Architecture. Sensitive subjects—including victims, minors, death, illness, disability, abuse, poverty, allegations, grief, trauma, vulnerable groups, and active emergencies—retain protected space and dignity.

Teleprompter realization identifies Romanian pronunciation, proper-name, institution, acronym, number, quotation, parenthetical, clitic, backtracking, and breath-group burdens. It never formats output.

## Bounded catalogues

The canonical pattern catalogue covers direct statements, fronting, inversion, ellipsis, fragments, repetition, connectors, repair, reflective closure, artificial formality, press/bureaucratic/academic formulas, calques, forced slang, artificial fragmentation, and overexplained payoff. Examples are isolated fragments under 100 characters.

The lexical catalogue is deliberately small and representative. Borrowings are not automatically rejected and linguistic purism is not enforced. AI-likeness indicators identify synthetic tendencies for editorial review; they never claim AI authorship and do not implement AI-text detection.

## Corrections and profile guidance

The correction handoff preserves original and edited references, explanation, category, scope, permanence intent, and provenance for future Module 2.7C. It performs no analysis, learning, persistence, or canonical mutation. Permanent project rules require explicit editor permanence.

Only established or explicitly confirmed editor guidance may tune active assessments. Observed, emerging, deprecated, and rejected guidance remains non-operative. Local corrections remain local. Guidance cannot override facts, attribution, law, sensitivity, or any upstream editorial system.

## Assessment, readiness, and lineage

Assessments preserve semantic fingerprints for the engine, Spoken Communication Assessment, and Story Architecture Plan, plus upstream Persona, Philosophy, Voice, Audience, and Decision identities.

Readiness precedence is blocked, requires editor review, ready with advisories, then ready. Upstream blocking and review states propagate. Severe ambiguity, legal distortion, sensitivity violations, and critical risks block; contextual register, marked forms, unstable slang, and major burdens require review; minor cadence and artificiality findings remain advisories.

Renderers and SHA-256 fingerprints are deterministic and UTF-8 safe. Principle, register, and ranked-pattern order remains meaningful; unordered evidence, risk, guidance, episode, and correction references normalize. No timestamps, paths, runtime state, AI, network, persistence, CLI, benchmarks, prompt mutation, generated prose, or replacement wording are introduced.
