# Editorial Intent

## Responsibilities and contract

Intent records why an edit exists independently of its operation, using a stable ID, controlled category, and explanation reference. Graph operations and observations reference it and validators enforce that lineage.

## Dependencies, limitations, and guarantees

Intent is reusable, immutable, versioned, renderable, and fingerprintable. It never contains source, generated, or replacement language and is never inferred from AI or unvalidated output.
