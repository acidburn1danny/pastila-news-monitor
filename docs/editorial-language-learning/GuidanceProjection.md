# Guidance Projection

## Responsibilities and contract

Projection exposes established and explicit preference IDs, confidence, status, scope, compatibility, dimensions, and review requirements in stable order. It references its source profile and preferences.

## Boundaries and guarantees

Guidance is advisory. Inactive/rejected/deprecated/archived preferences are not projected. It contains no recommendation prose or replacement wording, edits no text, widens no scope, and cannot override upstream systems.
