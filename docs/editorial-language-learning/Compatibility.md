# Compatibility

## Responsibilities and contract

Editorial Memory, Persona, Philosophy, Decision Framework, Voice, Audience, Story Architecture, Spoken Communication, and Romanian Conversation are immutable upstream systems. Snapshots capture each canonical ID, semantic version, SHA-256 fingerprint, and readiness.

## Dependencies, limitations, and guarantees

Snapshots contain references rather than upstream objects. Duplicate identities, malformed identity data, or canonical mutation are rejected and blocked upstream readiness propagates. Learning cannot modify upstream rules, rendering, fingerprints, or ownership.
