# Preference Lifecycle

## Responsibilities and contract

Allowed paths are candidate → emerging → established → deprecated → archived, candidate → rejected, explicit_editor_rule → established, and established → superseded. All other transitions fail deterministically.

## Editor authority, limitations, and guarantees

The Editor-in-Chief may explicitly restore deprecated → established through the authorization boundary. Inference cannot restore or override decisions. Deprecated, rejected, archived, and superseded artifacts retain evidence and predecessor/successor lineage.
