# Editorial Language Profile

## Responsibilities and contract

The profile partitions preferences into active, emerging, explicit, deprecated, rejected, and archived buckets and includes conflicts, derived confidence/maturity, counters, explanation lineage, identity/version, and fingerprint.

## Dependencies, limitations, and guarantees

`build_profile` consumes accepted artifacts, sorts IDs, and never promotes candidates. Validation checks IDs, bucket/status agreement, evidence, counts, and fingerprint. Generated material is forbidden and inactive history remains visible.
