# Editor Editorial Mechanics V1

PastilaAcida Editor uses one provider-neutral, structured mechanics authority for
normal stories and transitions. A story orients the audience in at most two
factual-setup sentences, gives the majority of its planned space to acid
commentary, and ends with an editorial landing; a serious reset remains valid.
A transition is a separate editorial unit, prefers one sentence, permits at most
two, and acts as a punchline or meaningful bridge rather than a mechanical news
announcement.

STANDARD remains `story-word-budget-v2`: target 190 and hard maximum 210. Its
deterministic planning guidance remains 47 factual-summary words, 108 commentary
words, and 35 ending words. These component allocations are not validation
ceilings. Transitions retain their separate budget.

The current runtime prompt projects the mechanics as compact typed data. Facts,
intent, safety, schema, budgets, and episode state must remain dynamic runtime
authority. Stable voice, rhythm, commentary-first balance, anti-newsreader
behavior, and landing/transition instincts are future
`WEIGHT_INTERNALIZATION_CANDIDATE` behavior; this version neither trains a model
nor promotes learned preferences.

Editorial Evidence may record explicit mechanic identities as observations.
Missing mechanic metadata remains valid for historical captures, and recording
an observation never changes generation behavior.
