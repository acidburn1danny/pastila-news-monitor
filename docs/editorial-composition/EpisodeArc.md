# EpisodeArc

EpisodeArc is the episode-wide structural trajectory. It binds every ordinary segment to exactly one primary ArcStep while preserving the authoritative Composition Plan order. It is distinct from segment ordering, ToneProgression, TransitionPlan, and CallbackPlan.

Controlled functions are orientation, build, escalation, peak, stabilization, relief, reset, reflection, resolution, and closure. `custom:<slug>` requires valid controlled syntax and traceable authority. Structural intensity is low, moderate, elevated, high, or peak and does not predict audience emotion.

## Stabilization

Stabilization consolidates or clarifies already introduced material after complexity or pressure. It is not relief, reset, reflection, or resolution. It does not lower gravity, imply resolution, hide facts, bypass attribution/legal constraints, reassure emotionally, or act as filler. It must preserve unresolved constraints and have a traceable structural reason.

Example: peak → stabilization → reflection, with serious tone, high gravity, consolidation of confirmed facts, preserved unresolved constraints, and no generated language.

Invalid structure, order, bindings, dignity/sensitivity violations, hidden facts, false resolution, missing traceability, or bad fingerprints block readiness. Ambiguous structural choices require editor review.
