# Semantic Admission V2 — staged Gate F feasibility design

## Outcome

A two-stage Gate F architecture is feasible as an evaluation design, with important limits. It separates proposition discovery from governed classification and makes omissions and source grounding visible. It does not make model silence safe and does not convert the intermediate ledger into factual authority.

## Stage P: proposition ledger

Stage P inventories every real-world commitment carried by assertion, presupposition, entailment, or necessary implication. It also records contained creative material and unresolved scope. It cannot decide admission or emit FSEM codes.

An empty ledger cannot be marked complete. Fact-safe commentary must still ground the creative or editorial material that explains why no unsupported real-world commitment was extracted. Any unresolved scope makes Stage P indeterminate.

A new separately versioned constrained grammar is required because the existing Gate F decision grammar cannot represent this ledger without conflating extraction and admission.

## Deterministic boundary

Deterministic validation is deliberately narrow: schema, enums, entry/group references, exact source membership, completeness invariants, and identities. It cannot infer missing propositions, assign semantic codes, or repair evidence.

Any invalid or indeterminate Stage P result abstains before Stage C and consumes only one call.

## Stage C: classification and completeness audit

Stage C receives the original immutable authority, original candidate, and validated-but-untrusted ledger. It must independently audit completeness. Ledger omission is never proof of safety. Suspected omission or unresolved completeness produces indeterminate abstention.

For a complete ledger, Stage C classifies unsupported propositions, compares event axes before open-class labels, groups dimensions of one proposition, and assigns decisive/supporting status. Existing strict Gate F output grammar may be retained only if zero-inference compatibility passes.

## Calls and precedence

The hard maximum is two model calls per candidate, with no retries. Stage P failure or indeterminacy stops after one. A future two-case proof would therefore permit at most four calls.

Every raw provider response must be stored before validation. All stage receipts and the aggregate precedence receipt remain quarantined evaluation evidence.

## Proof contract

The first proof remains Cases 01 and 10:

- Case 01: grounded contained-creative ledger, complete coverage, then exact PASS.
- Case 10: grounded certainty/timing and life-stakes commitments, correct grouping, then certainty decisive with timing and stakes supporting—no causality or motive.

Both cases must match exactly before any ten-case run becomes eligible.

## Authority

Design only. No schema, grammar, prompt, executor, validator, inference, runtime, Gate S, curriculum, or training implementation is authorized.
