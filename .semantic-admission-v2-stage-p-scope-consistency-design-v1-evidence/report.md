# Stage P Case 01 overlap and scope-consistency design

The frozen V2 ledger fails because it both literalizes three components of one integrated figurative construction and classifies that complete construction as carrying no real-world commitment. Existing spans and independence groups cannot represent or reconcile this relationship.

The design does not ban overlap. Genuine factual clauses and unsupported presuppositions can occur inside creative hosts. Instead, the proposed `STAGE_P_SCOPE_GRAPH_V1` requires an explicit host relation and a factual-return basis showing that the alleged proposition survives removal of the creative vehicle. Mere literal paraphrase of metaphorical words is not a real-world proposition.

Case 01's three components fail the factual-return test; the integrated shadow/light/room/transparency transformation remains contained creative. Mixed governed and unsupported embedded examples remain representable and fail closed when scope cannot be reconciled.

Recommended next step: implement the evaluation-only scope-graph schema and construction fixtures, then stop for owner review before prompt, runner, or inference work.
